Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who is responsible

This is the personal portfolio of Mariam Abdelaleem. For anything about your data, email mariam.abdelaleem@gmail.com.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, browser and time of visit to deliver the site and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. More: GitHub Privacy Statement.

No tracking, no cookies

This site uses no analytics, no advertising and no cookies of its own. Fonts are stored on this site, so no font requests go to Google.

Your browser's session storage remembers for one visit that you have already seen the intro. This stays on your device and is deleted when you close the tab.

YouTube videos

The music video cards show preview images from YouTube (i.ytimg.com, Google), which load when you scroll to that section, so Google receives your IP address at that point. The videos themselves are not loaded until you press play. Then the video is loaded from YouTube (Google Ireland Limited) in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube then also receives data about your viewing. More: Google Privacy Policy.

Contact form and links

The "Ask Mariam" assistant runs entirely in your browser; your questions are not sent anywhere. The contact form opens your own email app with a prepared message, so nothing is sent until you send that email yourself. If you email me, I use your message only to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Links to LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube and Spotify open those sites, where their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data, to data portability and to object (Art. 15–21 GDPR). You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
